My Therapeutic Focus

I work with adults individually or as couples, and children with their parents or within their families. Together we will decide what course of treatment will be most beneficial; a decision which is based on your unique needs.

I am able to offer clinical expertise in, and enjoy working on the following concerns:

  • Depression and Mood Disruptions
  • Anxiety and Fears
  • Grief and Loss
  • Shame and Guilt
  • Dating and Relationship Difficulties
  • Low Self-Esteem, Confidence, and Body Issues
  • Conflict Resolution
  • Stress and Anger Management
  • Trauma Recovery
  • Work and Career Goals
  • School and Academic Concerns
  • Creative Blocks and Life Transitions

I provide a supportive and non-judgmental space in which all sexual identities, genders, styles of relationships, races, ethnicities, religions, and immigration statuses are welcome and treated with respect.
